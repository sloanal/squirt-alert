How the model works

Capacity. Studies of men aged 40–44 report average ejaculate volumes of about 2.5–3.4 mL. Reserve uses 3.0 mL as "full". Most of that fluid comes from the seminal vesicles and prostate. The testes contribute the sperm, which is only around 5% of the volume.

Release. Each logged release drains about 90% of the current reserve.

Refill. Refill follows an exponential curve with a time constant of about 1.6 days. That works out to roughly 50% after 1 day, about 85% after 3 days and about 98% after 6 days. These figures match MRI data showing the seminal vesicles refill over about 3 days, and abstinence studies where volume rises from about 1.8 mL at 1 day to about 3.5 mL at 6 or more days. "Full" means 95%.

This is an estimate for fun and general awareness, not a medical device.
